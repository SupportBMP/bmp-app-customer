## Data safety form (based on what the app does)

- **Personal info:** name, phone number, email address, home address. Collected for account management and booking. Not shared for advertising.
- **Location:** approximate and precise location, used to suggest your city and fill in the address. Optional.
- **Financial info:** card and UPI payments go through Razorpay. The app never stores card details. Declare "Purchase history" for orders.
- **Photos:** only if the user uploads one (image picker). Optional.
- **App activity / device IDs:** the session token and device ID (X-MUID) are used for app functionality.
- Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS): **Yes**.
- Users can ask for their data to be deleted: **Yes** (give a deletion URL or email; Play requires one for apps with accounts).
